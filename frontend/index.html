The 3-Step Ceremony

The VÉRRA Hair Ceremony

Transform hair washing into a restorative sensory retreat with our three-stage mindful hair ritual.

01

Scalp Awakening

Part hair into quadrants. Dispense 1-2 droppers of Root Revival Oil onto dry scalp. Perform circular lymphatic massage for 5 minutes to stimulate microcirculation.

02

Cortex Shield

Coat fragile lengths with Coco Silk Treatment or Botanical Nourish Oil 30 minutes before cleansing. Wraps cuticles to prevent hygral fatigue and protein depletion.

03

Silk Gloss Sealing

Warm 2-3 drops of Silk Elixir between warm palms and glaze over damp or dry mid-lengths for weightless humidity protection and instant glass-like luminosity.
