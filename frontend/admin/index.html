Executive BI Metric Definitions & Formulas

Academic Business Intelligence Standards

1. Gross Sales vs Net Revenue

Gross Sales = Sum of all product order items before discounts (UnitPrice × Quantity).
Net Revenue = Gross Sales - Order Discounts (VERRA10/RITUAL15). Excludes cancelled and pending orders.

2. COGS & Gross Profit

Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) = Sum of UnitCostSnapshot × Quantity for qualifying fulfilled orders.
Gross Profit = Net Revenue - COGS.
Gross Margin % = (Gross Profit ÷ Net Revenue) × 100.

3. Net Operating Profit & OpEx

Operating Expenses (OpEx) = Sum of categorized expenses: Marketing, Packaging, Carrier Shipping Subsidies, Fulfilment, and Admin.
Net Operating Profit = Gross Profit - Operating Expenses.
Operating Margin % = (Net Operating Profit ÷ Net Revenue) × 100.

4. Average Order Value (AOV) & Refund Rate

Average Order Value (AOV) = Net Revenue ÷ Qualifying Orders Count.
Refund Rate = (Refunded Orders ÷ Total Placed Orders) × 100.

5. Inventory Runway & Turnover

Inventory Valuation = Sum of StockQuantity × UnitCost for all active formulations.
Daily Run Rate = Past 30-Day Units Sold ÷ 30 Days.
Stock Runway = Current Stock Quantity ÷ Daily Run Rate (Days remaining until stockout).
Annualized Turnover = (Monthly COGS × 12) ÷ Total Stock Valuation.
